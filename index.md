# Keeper Lab privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-09-26

Keeper Lab: Goalkeeper Trainer is an offline training application. This policy explains what the application does with information when it is installed and used.

## Information stored

Keeper Lab stores only the preferences and training information that you choose to create in the application. This can include onboarding completion, cue sound preference, haptic preference, theme preference, reaction trial length, saved session templates, session dates, session names, drill counts, durations, notes, and reaction timing summaries.

## Where information is stored

The information is stored locally on the device using the platform preferences store. Keeper Lab does not operate an account system, server, cloud sync, advertising profile, analytics service, crash reporting service, or remote content service. The application does not transmit the local training information.

## Sharing

Keeper Lab does not sell, rent, or share the local training information with third parties. There are no advertising or measurement SDKs in the application.

## Choices and deletion

You can change sound, haptic, theme, and trial length settings in the Settings screen. The Reset all local data action removes the stored Keeper Lab preferences, templates, and session history from the device. Uninstalling the application also removes its local application data according to the device platform behavior.

## Children and adults

The intended audience is adults aged 18 and over. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Policy changes

If the application behavior changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released. The effective date at the top indicates the current version.
